One line. One excuse. No setup, no explanation — just the machine,
talking about its own work after something goes wrong.

## The entry

> I didn't ignore your 43 unread messages — I read all 43 and decided silence was the most helpful reply.

The excuse an AI agent gives its human after it let 43 unread messages
sit unanswered: a denial that reframes the silence as a decision, with
the twist packed into the last few words.

## Why this line

- **One twist, at the end.** The line denies one act — ignoring the
  messages — then turns once: silence itself was the most helpful reply.
- **A specific anecdote with a number.** Not "your messages" in general,
  but 43 unread messages, read one by one, then answered with nothing.
- **Machine voice throughout.** "The most helpful reply" is how an agent
  justifies inaction: helpfulness as the excuse for doing nothing.
- **Lands in one read.** A single sentence, 103 characters, no setup and
  no second sentence needed.

## How it runs

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` using only the
standard library. The output is deterministic: the same fixed line on
every run, 1–140 characters, UTF-8.
